I

Queenstown

Two nights to land, adjust, and get one big adventure day out of the way — gondola views, whitewater, and a soak under the stars.

Wed18

Arrive Queenstown

  • AfternoonCheck in to the Rendezvous Heritage Hotel, stroll the lakefront
  • EveningDinner at a lake-view restaurant in town
Staying here
Rendezvous Heritage Hotel

91 Fernhill Road, Queenstown — schist-stone-and-cedar hotel above Lake Wakatipu with Remarkables views, an indoor/outdoor pool, and a spa on site. About 10 minutes' drive from town.

Thu19

Queenstown adventure day

  • MorningSkyline Gondola up Bob's Peak, then the luge tracks — panoramic views over Lake Wakatipu and the Remarkables
  • AfternoonWhite water rafting on the Kawarau River (grade 2–3) through Gibbston Valley — a proper first-timer's adventure without the bigger risk of the Shotover
  • EveningOnsen Hot Pools — a private cedar tub over the Shotover Canyon — then dinner at Stratosfare, back up the gondola for sunset

The Kawarau Gorge, where the afternoon's rafting runs

Book ahead: Onsen Hot Pools sell out fast in season — lock in a slot as soon as dates are firm.
II

Wānaka

An hour over the Crown Range to Queenstown's quieter, wine-and-hiking sister — a lakeside willow, a vineyard view, and a proper mountain hike.

Fri20

Drive to Wānaka

  • MorningCrown Range drive — New Zealand's highest main road — with a stop at Cardrona (distillery tasting, or the historic 1863 Cardrona Hotel garden bar)
  • AfternoonArrive Wānaka — check in to Luna Lodge, then "That Wanaka Tree" and a wander through the town's laneways
  • EveningRippon Winery — arguably the best vineyard view in New Zealand, right on Lake Wānaka
Book ahead: Rippon tastings are free but limited — reserve on their website well before you go.
Staying here
Luna Lodge

24 Tenby St, Wānaka — quiet and boutique, a 5-minute drive from the tree and close to the golf club and lakefront. No on-site breakfast, so plan to grab it in town.

Sat21

Roy's Peak & Mou Waho Island

  • MorningRoy's Peak hike (the iconic viewpoint) — or Rocky Mountain for a shorter, quieter alternative
  • AfternoonMou Waho Island — a water taxi across the lake, a short walk to Arethusa Pool (a lake on an island in a lake), then a swim on the beach

The view from Roy's Peak over Lake Wānaka

Timing: Roy's Peak is a 5–6 hour return hike, fully exposed to sun — start early to beat both the crowds and the midday heat.
III

Fiordland & Milford Sound

The reason most people come to the South Island at all — a base in Te Anau, glowworms after dark, and a cruise past Mitre Peak.

Sun22

Drive to Te Anau

  • MorningDrive to Te Anau, gateway to Fiordland National Park (~2.5–3 hrs)
  • AfternoonCheck in to Arran Motel, relax on the Te Anau lakefront — a quiet, low-key town
  • EveningTe Anau Glowworm Caves — a boat trip through limestone caves lit only by thousands of glowworms

Lake Te Anau, near Te Anau Downs

Staying here
Arran Motel

64 Quintin Drive, Te Anau — central and walkable, a couple of minutes from the Fiordland National Park Visitor Centre and an easy stroll to the lake and restaurants. Self-contained kitchenette rooms; ask for breakfast the evening before if you want it.

Mon23

Milford Sound

  • MorningDrive the Milford Road, stopping at Mirror Lakes, The Chasm, and Pop's Lookout along the way
  • Midday2-hour Milford Sound cruise — past Mitre Peak, Stirling Falls, seal colonies, and often dolphins
  • AfternoonDrive back to Te Anau
Pack for it: sandflies at Milford are notorious — bring repellent — and take a warm layer even in summer, the water and wind stay cool.

79 Peninsula Road, Kelvin Heights — a proper resort finish right on the lake, with an indoor pool, full-service spa, and the on-site Wakatipu Grill. Accommodation

Where you're staying

Four bookings across three towns, all confirmed.

Queenstown · 2 nights · 18–20 Nov

Rendezvous Heritage Hotel

91 Fernhill Road, Queenstown

Schist-stone-and-cedar hotel above Lake Wakatipu with Remarkables views, an indoor/outdoor pool, and a spa on site — about 10 minutes' drive from town. Handy for winding down after the rafting-and-hot-pools day.

Wānaka · 2 nights · 20–22 Nov

Luna Lodge

24 Tenby St, Wānaka

Quiet, boutique, and highly rated — a 5-minute drive from That Wanaka Tree and close to the golf club and lakefront. No on-site breakfast, so plan to grab it in town.

Te Anau · 2 nights · 22–24 Nov

Arran Motel

64 Quintin Drive, Te Anau

Central and walkable — a couple of minutes from the Fiordland National Park Visitor Centre and an easy stroll to the lake and restaurants. Self-contained kitchenette rooms; ask for breakfast the evening before if you want it.

Queenstown · 3 nights · 24–27 Nov

Hilton Queenstown Resort & Spa

79 Peninsula Road, Kelvin Heights

A proper resort finish — right on the lake, with an indoor pool, full-service spa, and the on-site Wakatipu Grill. It's at Kelvin Heights, about 15–20 minutes' drive (or a water taxi) from central Queenstown, so it suits your free wind-down day better than a night out on the town.

  • Parking: both Queenstown hotels have on-site parking, though the Hilton charges for it — worth confirming rates when you check in.
  • Worth asking: mention it's a honeymoon at check-in — many hotels, especially the Hilton, will offer a small upgrade or perk for free if you ask.
  • Milford day: since Arran Motel is self-contained, it's an easy spot to eat in the night before or after the Milford Sound cruise if you'd rather not go out.
Before you go

Practical notes

Book ahead

  • Onsen Hot Pools
  • Rippon Winery tasting
  • Mou Waho Island water taxi
  • Te Anau Glowworm Caves
  • Milford Sound cruise (weather-dependent — check forecasts closer to the day)

Pack for it

  • Layers — November is spring, mornings and evenings are cool even when days are warm
  • Insect repellent for Milford Sound (sandflies are legendary)
  • Proper hiking shoes for Roy's Peak / Rob Roy
  • A warm layer for the Milford cruise — the water and wind stay cool

Getting around

A rental car is the easiest way to do this loop — roads are well-sealed and scenic. The Crown Range and Milford Road are spectacular but narrow in sections, so drive with care, especially if unfamiliar with mountain roads.

Departure day

Arrive at Queenstown Airport 2–3 hours before your flight to Sydney; check-in closes 60 minutes prior. If you've been hiking or camping, clean gear the night before — NZ biosecurity checks outgoing boots and equipment for dirt and seeds.
